How to play
  • ← → or A D to move. On a phone, tap the left or right half, swipe, or use the buttons.
  • Every car drives differently: check its speed, acceleration and handling. The launcher fires you off the start.
  • Dodge cones, rival cars and holes: every crash costs a life (you start with 3). Oil makes you skid and the rails throw sparks.
  • Grab coins 🟡 and nitro bolts ⚡ to go faster and smash through cones.
  • Jump between tables and fly through the middle of the fire hoop. Banked curves hold you in and loops spin you around by themselves.
  • Every level is faster and is another room of the house: bedroom, living room, kitchen and garden. Space or P pauses.
  • Reach 2,000, 5,000, 10,000 and 20,000 m in a single run to unlock four more cars. Number 99 starts with 5 lives.
  • If your score makes the top 20, leave your nickname in the worldwide ranking. The sound button keeps music and engine apart from the effects.
